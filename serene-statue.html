Serene Statue

Photogrammetry · Solo · Nikon P510 → RealityScan → Blender → Unreal Engine 5

At a Glance

Serene Statue is my first photogrammetry asset: a carved wooden bust that I photographed, rebuilt as a 3D model, cleaned up, and brought into Unreal Engine 5 as a Nanite mesh. It was a weekend project to learn the whole pipeline, from camera to engine.

Capture

I took 96 photos with a Nikon P510 in my greenhouse, where the plastic walls give soft, even light. The statue sat on a turntable, and I shot three rings at different heights, turning it a little between each photo.

RealityScan

My first try wouldn't align. The statue turned while the background stayed still, which confused RealityScan about where the camera was. Masking the photos with its AI masking tool fixed that, so only the statue was used. From there it was align, build the model at normal detail, simplify, unwrap, texture, and export.

Triangles and Nanite

The raw scan came out at about 2 million triangles: lots of detail, but dense and uneven. I simplified it to about 1 million, which kept the silhouette and the carved detail. In Unreal, Nanite groups triangles into clusters and only draws the detail each pixel needs, so a mesh this heavy still runs in real time.

Cleanup in Blender

The scan picked up a sliver of the turntable under the base. In Blender I used Bisect with Clear Inner and Fill to cut the bottom flat and close it, moved the origin to the bottom so it sits properly on surfaces in engine, and exported it as an FBX.

In Unreal Engine

I imported the FBX with Nanite turned on and placed the statue in my scenes to see how it holds up next to other assets and lighting.

What I'd Do Differently

  • Add an even lower ring of photos, under the chin, to pick up the underside.
  • Put a plain backdrop behind the turntable, so there's less background for the software to sort out.
  • Get closer, so the statue fills more of each frame and every photo carries more detail.

Next, I want to retopologize it and bake the high-poly detail onto a lighter version.
